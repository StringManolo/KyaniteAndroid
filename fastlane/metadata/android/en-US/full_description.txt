A Termux alternative that gives you a small, memory-safe Kyanite userland on Android.

- Kyanite under proot, using the signed Kyanite rootfs release
- Floating-terminal-friendly UI with ESC/TAB/CTRL/ALT/HOME/END/arrow keys
- System and LiveCD environments, each in three modes: Normal (persistent), Ephemeral (wiped on exit) and VM (QEMU, RAM only)
