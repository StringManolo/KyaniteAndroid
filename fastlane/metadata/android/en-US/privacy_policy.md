# Privacy Policy – Kyanite for Android

_Last updated: November 27, 2026_

Kyanite for Android ("the app") is developed by StringManolo. This policy explains what the app does with your data. Short version: **it does not collect your data.**

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no trackers. The developer receives no information about you or about how you use the app.

## What stays on your device

Your Linux systems, snapshots, settings, PIN settings and any encryption keys are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. If you enable encryption, systems are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and the key is either generated by the app and kept in the Android Keystore, or derived from a passphrase that you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## When the app uses the network

The app only connects to the internet when you ask it to:

- **Downloading a system:** when you choose to download a root filesystem (Kyanite, Alpine Linux, Ubuntu Base, Arch Linux ARM, or a URL you type), the app downloads it from the server you picked. That server can see your IP address and the request, like any web server.
- **Updates (off by default):** if you turn on update checks, the app contacts GitHub (github.com / api.github.com) to look for a new release and, if you allow it, to download the APK. Android always asks you to confirm an installation.
- **Programs you run:** programs that you run inside your Linux system (for example `apt`, `curl` or a browser) use the network on their own terms, and their privacy is up to them. You can turn the network off for everything running in the app with the network switch in Settings.

The network switch uses Android's VPN interface to create a local VPN that has no exit: traffic is dropped on the device and is never sent to any server.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** downloading systems and, if enabled, checking for updates.
- **VPN service:** the local network switch described above.
- **Display over other apps:** the optional floating window.
- **Foreground service, notifications and wake lock:** keeping a session running and, optionally, keeping the CPU awake while it runs.
- **Receive boot completed:** scheduling background maintenance, such as the checks of the optional scheduled wipe.
- **Install packages:** installing an update that you downloaded from the app.
- **Storage:** optional access to shared storage from inside the Linux system, if you enable it.

## Data deletion

You can delete any system, snapshot or all app data at any time from the app (including the anti-forensic wipe) or by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to delete on a server.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app's repository with a new date.
